How it works

Press Call Jenn and allow microphone access. Speak through your mic and speakers — no account or phone number needed. Use Mute to pause your audio and End call when you’re done.

Jenn can’t hear you?

Check your microphone before calling. Speak for eight seconds and watch the input meter. This check stays on your device; it isn’t recorded or sent.

Use your browser’s site controls to allow the microphone and select the correct input. Check that your headset is unmuted and your computer’s input volume is up. Close other call tabs, then try again.
